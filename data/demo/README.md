# Demonstration source extracts

This folder contains selected source-derived CSV extracts from a three-project
research demonstration. The records preserve source IDs and source URLs so a
reader can trace claims back to the cited public material.

Included files:
- `raw_sources.csv`
- `raw_projects.csv`
- `raw_organizations.csv`
- `raw_claims.csv`
- `raw_org_mentions.csv`

The extracts are a historical snapshot (research access dates are recorded in
the CSVs, primarily November 2026). They are not independently re-audited by
this repository. A source URL is a pointer to the publisher's material, not
proof that every extracted claim is correct or current.

Analyst-created pain-point and opportunity hypotheses are deliberately not
included in this portfolio data pack. Header-only templates are in
`../templates/`. The full PostgreSQL dump and internal decision history are
also excluded.

Public accessibility does not itself grant a right to republish a dataset.
Before making this repository public, verify publisher/site terms and obtain
any company approval required for the compiled dataset and research work.
