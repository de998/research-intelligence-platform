# Research evaluation methodology

The SQL worksheet organizes evidence into five dimensions. It is a structured
research aid, not an automated investment recommendation or universal scoring
model.

## 1. Stability

- **S1 — Approvals and compliance:** identify approval, oversight, and
  regulatory evidence.
- **S2 — Ownership and follow-up records:** distinguish source-reported
  participants from reviewed roles and current relationship status.

## 2. Profitability

- **F1 — Investment and costs:** examine investment plans, procurement scope,
  and cost evidence.
- **F2 — Funding and participants:** compare funding disclosures, named
  participants, and source documents.
- **F3 — Payment history and present value:** search for payment, funding,
  revenue, settlement, and present-value evidence.
- **F4 — Financial outlook and exposure:** assemble relevant financial claims
  and related operational/commercial qualifications.

## 3. Longevity

- **L1 — Stage and milestones:** distinguish the research snapshot from
  milestones and forecast dates.
- **L2 — Operations and maintenance:** examine maintenance scope and
  participant responsibilities.
- **L3 — Long-term availability:** compare asset dependencies, maintenance
  commitments, and reported concerns.

## 4. Feasibility and viability

- **V1 — Demand and use:** look for demand, utilization, orders, throughput,
  and operational evidence.
- **V2 — Capability gaps:** inspect dependencies, delivery capability, and
  coordination responsibilities.
- **V3 — Evidence for alternative solutions:** retrieve the factual baseline
  needed to compare possible approaches.

## 5. Commercial opportunities

- **O1 — Pain points:** record problems separately from their evidence class.
- **O2 — Service and value hypotheses:** describe potential services as
  hypotheses unless validated.
- **O3 — Buyer and procurement validation:** check buyer identity, budget,
  purchasing authority, and purchase intent.
- **O4 — Fee and delivery effort validation:** assess commercial economics and
  delivery effort before treating a service idea as viable.

## Interpretation rules

- Missing information is unknown; it is not automatically a negative finding.
- A public announcement, tender estimate, candidate award, forecast, and
  operational result are different evidence types.
- A project-quality assessment and a consulting opportunity assessment answer
  different questions.
- Commercial hypotheses should not influence the objective assessment of the
  underlying project.
- A pain-point/opportunity relationship requires an explicit human decision
  supported by rationale and evidence.
- The worksheet uses case-specific IDs in its SQL routing. Update and review
  those mappings before using it on another research case.
